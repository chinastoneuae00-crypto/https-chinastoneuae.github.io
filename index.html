ABOUT CHINA STONE

More Than Construction,
We Build Trust.

China Stone is a leading construction and stone company, providing high-quality construction and stone services. We focus on quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction. China Stone constrution is a professional stone, tiling and all masonry works establishment. China Stone started its business in 1997 by associating with a local company and established its own business in the year of 2003. It is a professional service provider in the masonry Construction business sector. We work as construction partners and we assure the quality in each stage of work and ensure success of our partners in terms of execution with the support of our 500 trained professionals work force. During the last 21 years we have executed and completed successfully a range of projects on the basis of supply and fix marble works as well as block work, ceramic tiling work by our staff. Some of these projects are outstanding in Dubai and Abu Dhabi such as: Presidential Palace Stone and Mosaic Work Palazzo Versace Marble and Mosaic Works Al Wahda Mall Extension screed, tiling block works Swimming Pool in Mureena Raha Beach Golf Club Resort Landscape Meydan Concourse and Water Features and Hard Cape Dubai Sidra Tower plastering, Tiling Dubai Motor City Showroom tiling work Ministry of Interior Building External Stone Cladding Abu Dhabi Sheikh Zayed Mosque External Paving work UAE Central Bank Building Granite Work National Bank of Abu Dhabi on Khalifa Street Granite and Marble Cladding Palm Island Outdoor Swimming Pool work Burj Dubai Residence Phase I, Phase II and Phase III Tiling Works Al Ain Municipality Extension Office Building Block Work and Tiling Work Sheikh Hashar Al Maktoom Palace Stone Work Sheikh Khalifa Palace Landscape Tiling Work. Workmanship is always the first concern of China Stones. During the recruitment of every recruiter are carefully checked and tested, and professional training of one of our emphasized work to meet all the demands of our clients. Armed with all machinery power tools and all essential tools, China Stone enjoys good reputation in management and workmanship as well as execution timing. We would like to take this opportunity to extent our best regards to all of our Clients who offered us the opportunities to serve them. We look forward to serve our new and old clients with our sincerity and experience.
